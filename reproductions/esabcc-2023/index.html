    ESABCC 2023 reproduction¶

    The fair-share numbers in the ESABCC advice on the EU 2040 target (ESABCC 2023) come from an IIASA study, Pelz, Rogelj and Riahi (2023), here PRR2023 (report, replication code). Notebook 601_reproduce_esabcc_2023 runs the same approaches with the library and current data.

    In short:

    • Notebook 601 uses the PRR2023 settings through a preset, esabcc_2023_allocations(). The library defaults differ.
    • The global budget is smaller than in PRR2023: 1331 Gt from 1990 (PRR2023: 1530 Gt) and 651 Gt from 2015 (PRR2023: 704 Gt). Two choices cause most of this: the years before 2020 are added as fossil CO2 only, and international aviation and shipping are deducted.
    • The EU27 shares match PRR2023 closely; see Reproduction accuracy.
    • Notebook 601 also extends PRR2023 to land-use CO2 and the other greenhouse gases, and to budgets remaining from today.

    The details follow in the boxes below. Remaining carbon budgets, Scenario sets and Greenhouse gases explain the budget corrections, the scenario sets and the gas treatment for every run.

    The library defaults differ: functional form "asinh", no 2050 population window, GDP year by year. A tool that presents the PRR2023 approaches takes its settings from the preset (Presets):

    Python
    from fair_shares.library.presets import esabcc_2023_allocations

allocations = esabcc_2023_allocations("co2-ffi")  # the PRR2023 matrix, 36 runs

    Notebook 601 uses one budget, ar6_2020 at 1.5°C and 50%. Its section 5 subtracts observed emissions and reports the budget remaining from 2026 (from 2025 for all-ghg, whose land-use data end in 2024). Its section 6 spreads each remaining budget into an annual pathway to 2100.

    Reproduction accuracy¶

    Notebook 601 reproduces PRR2023 closely. The table compares the EU27 share of the global budget, in per cent.

    Approach PRR2023 fair-shares Difference (pp)
    Equal per capita, from 1990 7.88 7.88 +0.01
    Equal per capita, from 2015 5.96 5.94 -0.02
    Equal cumulative per capita, from 1990 5.93 5.65 -0.28
    Equal cumulative per capita, from 2015 5.47 5.06 -0.41
    Capability, all variants -0.36 to +0.04
    Responsibility, all variants -1.77 to +0.31

    The cumulative per capita rows differ through the newer population projections. The responsibility rows differ most because fair-shares allocates to countries and sums them, while PRR2023 allocates to the EU27 as one region. With the same regional inputs, the library reproduces the PRR2023 results exactly.

    Extensions beyond PRR2023: land-use CO2 and other gases

    Notebook 601 also runs co2 (with land-use CO2 as national inventories report it), all-ghg-ex-co2-lulucf and all-ghg. A remaining carbon budget limits CO2 only, so the CO2 part is a budget and the non-CO2 part is an annual pathway; the library holds no single greenhouse-gas budget number. At 1.5°C and 50% the co2 budget from 2020 is 383 Gt (474 Gt minus a 91 Gt national-inventory convention gap). The all-GHG categories start in 2015 in notebook 601; all-ghg cannot start before 2000, the first year of national-inventory land-use data. Greenhouse gases explains the method and the sources behind it.
