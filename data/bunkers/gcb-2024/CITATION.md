# Citation

## Global Carbon Budget 2024 — Fossil Carbon Emissions (Bunkers)

**Paper (methods and results):**

Friedlingstein, P., et al. (2025). Global Carbon Budget 2024. *Earth System Science Data*, 17, 965–1039. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-17-965-2025

**Data product (the spreadsheet in this directory):**

Global Carbon Project (2024). *Supplemental data of Global Carbon Budget 2024* (Version 1.0) [Data set]. https://doi.org/10.18160/GCP-2024

Cite both when reporting values derived from these files: the paper DOI is not the data DOI.

## Licence

The ESSD paper is CC BY 4.0. The **data product is not CC-licensed** — it carries the Global Carbon Project's own terms of use, which condition use on citing the original data sources and note that where the data are essential to a study, co-authorship may need to be considered. Treat redistribution of the raw spreadsheet as requiring GCP's terms rather than a Creative Commons grant.
