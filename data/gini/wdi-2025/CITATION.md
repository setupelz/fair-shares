# Citation

## World Bank Gini index

World Bank. (2025). Gini index (`SI.POV.GINI`). World Bank Open Data. https://data.worldbank.org/

Files:
- `API_SI.POV.GINI_DS2_en_csv_v2.csv` — Gini index, WDI indicator `SI.POV.GINI`

**Licence:** CC BY 4.0, the World Bank's default for its own open datasets
(https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses). No DOI is issued for this
indicator; cite by name as above.

**Provenance:** The World Bank sources this indicator from its own Poverty and
Inequality Platform (PIP). PIP reports consumption-based Gini for most low- and
middle-income countries and income-based Gini elsewhere, so values are not
directly comparable with income-based compilations such as WIID.

**Filename:** the bulk export names the CSV with a World Bank export-job number
(`..._v2_499.csv`) that changes on every regeneration. It is stored here under
the stable name above; the contents are unchanged.
