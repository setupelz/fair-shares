Friedlingstein, P., et al. (2026). Global Carbon Budget 2025. *Earth System Science Data*, 18, 3211-3288. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-18-3211-2026

## Files

- `GCB2025v15_MtCO2_flat.csv`: territorial fossil CO2 emissions by country, 1750-2024, long format. Units: MtCO2/yr. Also holds international shipping (`XIS`), international aviation (`XIA`), the 1991 Kuwaiti oil fires and the global total (`WLD`).
- `Global_Carbon_Budget_2025_v1.0.xlsx`: the global carbon budget, 1959-2024, in GtC/yr. The RCB step reads fossil emissions, the cement carbonation sink and bookkeeping land-use change emissions from the "Global Carbon Budget" sheet to express each budget from the start of 2020. Registry name `gcb-2025-global`.
